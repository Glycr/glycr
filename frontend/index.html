Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide (name, email, phone), payment data, and usage data when you interact with Glycr.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to process ticket purchases, send event notifications, improve our platform, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We share your data with event organizers (for events you attend), payment processors, and as required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact privacy@glycr.com.

6. Contact

Refund Policy

General Refund Policy

Refund eligibility is determined by the event organizer's policy set at the time of purchase. Glycr facilitates but does not guarantee refunds.

Event Cancellations

If an organizer cancels an event, all ticket holders are entitled to a full refund. Glycr will process these automatically within 5–7 business days.

Attendee-Initiated Refunds

Refund requests must be submitted through your Profile > Purchase History before the refund window closes.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days depending on payment method.

Cookie Policy

What are Cookies?

We use cookies for authentication, preferences, analytics, and to improve your experience.

Managing Cookies
